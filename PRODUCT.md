# SlowSleep

## Product truth

SlowSleep is a privacy-conscious bedtime companion. It helps a tired person make a small transition from the day into rest through either an OpenRouter-generated bedtime story or a browser-native ambient soundscape. A built-in story keeps the core experience usable when the network or server key is unavailable.

## Primary user

A person winding down alone at night who wants less stimulation, no account, and clear control over whether a story uses a cloud model. They may feel peaceful, worn out, restless, or hopeful.

## Product promise

Make room for rest without turning rest into another task.

## Surface mode

The primary surface is **Operate**: the visitor is completing a calming ritual. The interface should be quiet, legible, reassuring, and quick to use. Brand expression belongs in restrained typography, gentle contrast, and small moments of warmth rather than visual noise.

## Constraints

- Keep soundscapes and the built-in story usable without a network connection.
- OpenRouter is optional and must be called through the server, never directly from the browser.
- Soundscapes must be generated in the browser and stop cleanly.
- Never place an API key in browser state, localStorage, `VITE_*` variables, or the production bundle.
- Be explicit when a story was generated by OpenRouter versus the local fallback.
